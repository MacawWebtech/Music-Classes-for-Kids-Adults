Questions everyone asks

Talk to our team →
Do I need any musical experience?

Not at all. Most of our learners start from zero. Your teacher builds a plan around where you are today.

What ages do you teach?

We welcome young children, teens and adults, with age-appropriate batches for each.

Can we try a class before joining?

Yes. Book a free trial, meet a teacher and see which instrument feels right.

Do I need to own an instrument?

Not at first. You can practise on academy instruments while you decide what to buy.

Can I change my batch later?

Of course. Let us know and we will move you to a slot that suits your week.
